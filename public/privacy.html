Our lawful basis under UK GDPR is legitimate interests: we need this information to serve the site and protect it from abuse.

Who else sees it

Google processes request data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms, and may do so outside the UK using the safeguards set out in those terms. Google keeps it only as long as its own retention policies require. We do not sell or share it with anyone else.
